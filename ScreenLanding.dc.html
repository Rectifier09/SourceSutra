How onboarding works

Identity and Financials build on each other, in order. Portfolio can be filled in alongside either one, at your own pace — but it's the last piece needed to finish.

Step 1

Identity

Company details, contact verification, GST/PAN/MSME/CIN. Submitted first — Financials unlocks once this is in review.

Step 2

Financials

Bank details, addresses, and company filings. Locked until Identity is submitted for verification.

Alongside, then last

Portfolio

Work history, catalogue, and tags. Build it any time from day one — it's the final gate before onboarding is complete.
